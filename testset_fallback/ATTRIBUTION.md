# Test set: sources and licences

All clips are excerpts, resampled to 44.1 kHz, faded in/out (5 ms), normalized to -1 dBFS peak and decoded from Ogg Vorbis sources of github.com/librosa/data. Licences: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) or public domain, as listed.

- **speech_1.wav** (speech): Jane Austen, 'Sense and Sensibility' ch. 18, read by Heather Barnett; LibriSpeech SLR12 (via librosa/data), https://www.openslr.org/12/. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- **speech_2.wav** (speech): Marion Bryce, 'The Ashiel Mystery' ch. 16, read by Garth Comira; LibriSpeech SLR12 (via librosa/data), https://www.openslr.org/12/. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- **speech_3.wav** (speech): Thomas Bulfinch, 'The Age of Chivalry' ch. 9, read by Anders Lankford; LibriSpeech SLR12 (via librosa/data), https://www.openslr.org/12/. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- **tonal_trumpet.wav** (tonal): Mihai Sorohan, 'solo trumpet 06', https://freesound.org/s/77711/. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- **tonal_robin.wav** (tonal): InspectorJ, 'Bird Whistling, Robin, Single, 13', https://freesound.org/people/InspectorJ/sounds/456440/. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- **transient_snare_sparse.wav** (transient): Brian McFee, 'Accelerating snare drum' (snare sample by sakebeats, https://freesound.org/people/sakebeats/sounds/176345/), via librosa/data; synthetic pink noise floor added at -60 dB. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- **transient_snare_dense.wav** (transient): Brian McFee, 'Accelerating snare drum' (snare sample by sakebeats, https://freesound.org/people/sakebeats/sounds/176345/), via librosa/data; synthetic pink noise floor added at -60 dB. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- **poly_strings.wav** (polyphonic): J. Brahms, Hungarian Dance No. 5, performed by the US Army Strings, https://musopen.org/music/43805-hungarian-dance-no-5-in-f-sharp-minor-woo-1-string-orch. Licence: Public domain (CC PDM 1.0).
- **poly_orchestra.wav** (polyphonic): P. I. Tchaikovsky, 'Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairy', arranged by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), https://freemusicarchive.org/music/Kevin_MacLeod/Classical_Sampler/Dance_of_the_Sugar_Plum_Fairy. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- **poly_jazz.wav** (polyphonic): Kevin MacLeod, 'Vibe Ace' (incompetech.com), https://freemusicarchive.org/music/Kevin_MacLeod/Jazz_Sampler/Vibe_Ace. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- **noise_pink.wav** (noise): Synthetic pink noise, independent left/right channels (seed 1). Licence: none (synthetic).
- **noise_underwater.wav** (noise): Humpback whale song with underwater background noise, recorded by NPS - Chris Gabriele, Glacier Bay National Park, https://www.nps.gov/glba/learn/nature/soundclips.htm. Licence: Public domain (CC PDM 1.0).
- **stereo_panned.wav** (stereo): Derived from the Brahms recording above: mono mix panned (left 1.0, right 0.6), strongly correlated channels. Licence: Public domain (CC PDM 1.0).
- **stereo_phase.wav** (stereo): Derived from the Brahms recording above: mono mix, right channel delayed by 1 ms (44 samples), phase-shifted channels. Licence: Public domain (CC PDM 1.0).
